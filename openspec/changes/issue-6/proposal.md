## Problem

Specify shows the whole requirement, up to the first scenario. Plan and Design still stop at the first blank line. A Problem, an Outcome, or a design section with two paragraphs shows only the first one in the sidebar.

## Outcome

The Plan row shows the whole Problem section and the whole Outcome section. The Design row shows the whole text of each section it already shows. A section whose text is none stays none. Specify still shows the whole requirement body before the first scenario, including every WHEN and THEN.

## Scope

The Plan and Design rows of the open ticket. Plan shows the whole Problem section and the whole Outcome section. Design shows the whole text of each section that row already shows. This change is the sidebar in this repository and needs nothing from another service.

## Out of scope

Editing `proposal.md` or `design.md` from the sidebar. Any other rule the caller already sees on the board or in the sidebar.

## Constraints

A section whose text is none stays none. Specify keeps the whole requirement body before the first scenario, including every WHEN and THEN. The sidebar does not write the change files.
