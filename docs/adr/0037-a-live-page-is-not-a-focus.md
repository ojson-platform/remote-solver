# A live page is not a focus

The window computes the live page from the decision the spy settled and from whether a worker is on the cycle. The spy keeps the worker turn, the action the cap did not start, and that decision across a tracker refresh. The page is not a focus and not a history slot, and it leaves when the reason passes. On the gate for the proposal, the spec, the design, or accepting, now stays the written focus, including while a worker is writing that phase. The window does not settle. A wait is the decision's reason. An idle decision is not a full cap: the cap had room, and the same decision had already stopped. Keeping a past reason as a page was rejected: Back would open a reason that has passed. An issue comment or a focus marker was rejected: a focus is the skill's prose, and the window does not rewrite it. Treating the writing phase itself as that gate was rejected: the worker would have no page. Rebuilding the wait from host links, or from the plaque's `Waits for` line, was rejected: the decision already names who, and until which phase.
