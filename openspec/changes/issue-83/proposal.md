## Problem

Give the focus pager round chevrons. The characters `<` and `>` are not the mark.

## Outcome

The focus pager's back and forward controls are rounds, the same face as play on the card. Each round holds a chevron icon. The characters `<` and `>` are not the mark.

A round that cannot move stays in place and goes quiet, the way a disabled play does. The count stays between the two rounds. One written focus still leaves the pager out.
